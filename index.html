About

Andrew Vickers writes for the concert hall and for commercial media — commissions from One Found Sound, scores for NPR’s Snap Judgment, and music for brands including Hims and Sunski.

He holds an M.M. in composition from the San Francisco Conservatory of Music, and lives and teaches in Mill Valley, California.
